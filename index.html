How Texas Hold'em works

Win chips by making the best five-card hand from your two private cards and the five shared cards on the table, or by making everyone else fold.

Each hand

  1. Two players post forced bets called blinds. Everyone gets two private cards.
  2. Betting round. Then the flop: three shared cards.
  3. Betting round. Then the turn: one more shared card.
  4. Betting round. Then the river: the last shared card.
  5. Final betting round, then the showdown. The best hand wins the pot.

Your choices

  • Fold: give up the hand.
  • Check: pass when there is no bet to match.
  • Call: match the current bet.
  • Bet or raise: put in more chips. All in means betting everything you have.

Hand rankings, best to worst

  1. Straight flush: five in a row, same suit
  2. Four of a kind
  3. Full house: three of a kind plus a pair
  4. Flush: five cards of one suit
  5. Straight: five in a row
  6. Three of a kind
  7. Two pair
  8. One pair
  9. High card
